Jordan Oleson-Graves

Proposal: Potential Moiré Series

Site: Healey Building

Concept & Structure

This site-specific installation consists of 30" or 15" plywood panels joined at 90-degree angles. The structures are oriented with their hypotenuse base facing the windows, placed directly onto existing window ledge with additional supports under each corner (maximum depth of 24").

Window Graphics & Optics

The window glass features overlaid graphics designed to produce dynamic animating interference as viewers walk past. Depending on final direction, execution will utilize cut vinyl or printed window cling.

Design Variations

  • Glass Patterns: Due to vinyl cutter alignment constraints on long vertical runs, alternative patterns feature segmented color blocks and negative space holes to highlight the optical mechanism.
  • Rectangular vs. Organic: The window graphics could be rectangular formats or organic contours.

Project Timeline

Current models serve to communicate construction techniques and material interaction. Exact colors and patterns will be refined following initial direction approval.
